Save the two generated textures into THIS folder, named exactly:

    felt.png     the green table cloth
    rail.png     the dark wood

Then tell Claude they are here and it will resize, compress and wire
them in. Nothing else in the app needs to change.

Right now the table's felt and rail are drawn procedurally in CSS
(SVG fractal noise), which costs no download and cannot fail if a
CDN is down. These photographs are optional -- a swap, not a fix.
